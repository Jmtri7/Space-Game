Default story — culture design atlas

Bolted together from whatever a supply run can carry

A frontier trading and salvage outfit running the outposts at the edge of known space. Where the Vherathi grow their architecture, Drossholt bolts it together out of whatever plate and scavenged hull segments a supply run can carry — blunt, riveted, and built to be patched rather than beautiful.

The drossholt culture shipped (story 1.10.0) — the outpost and its moon rebuilt to the bolted floor-plan model, every hull / building / furniture silhouette extracted into parts. The hardware plates below are those shipped SVGs. The outfit redraws are a MOCKUP — the riveted patch-plates and box respirator are new figure geometry.

cultures.json → drossholt

  1. Blunt volumes. Rectangular masses bolted together along visible seams — no tapering or asymmetry for its own sake, the functional opposite of a grown structure.
  2. Rows on the seams. Windows and warning lights run in even, evenly-spaced rows along structural seams; thrusters and fixtures come in mirrored evenly-spaced pairs.
  3. Rivets and patches. Every edge studded with bolt ticks; a repair is a mismatched plate welded on, not hidden. Weathered, practical, patched.
  4. Right angles inside. Interiors are plain rectangles and wide straight corridors joined at right angles; every room edge studded with evenly-spaced bolt ticks.

Hardware — shipped

Ships (graphics.json), station (graphics.json → space_stations), buildings (building_types.json), furniture, decorations and both layouts are in config/stories/default, silhouettes extracted from these plates into parts by apply_parts.py.

Outfits — the redraw

Each outfit's recoloured base is a graphics.json → outfits entry (buildable now). The signature geometry is new — it needs a figure_signature table keyed by outfit id, fed the way gen_si.figure_parts already produces data. That one addition lands every culture's redraws at once.

The body

The cinched waist / rounded shoulders / belts-at-the-waist are shipped in person_figure.py. See the Common Kit atlas.
