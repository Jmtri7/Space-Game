The Grounded Person model

One proportion pass on the shared body in person_figure.py — a short torso over long legs (hip up at ~47% of height), shorter arms, bigger feet, a trapezius yoke that sweeps up behind the neck, rounded shoulders, a masc / femme split on the torso and hip width, and a flat one-direction shade down one side of each limb — then the face kit worked out on it: the egg-shaped head — tall rounded crown, high hairline, turned a shallow 3/4 toward the way the figure faces with the features clustered that side and a little smaller — the narrow oval eyes tilted up a touch at the outer corner, one short gently-arched brow, the small oval-shadow nose, a set of flat hairstyles that fit the head (plus a flush buzz-cut shadow) and skin tones, which way it faces, and how each holds up as the figure shrinks on screen.

1  The body

Head-count ticks up the left — about six and a half now, with the short torso over long legs. The top of the torso curves up behind the neck as a trapezius yoke, then sweeps down and out over the shoulder cap to meet the domed arm top in one smooth line. The waist pinches, then the hip curves back out — a convex flare, not a straight taper. The side shade is a strip that follows that curve.

Grounded · femme

Keeps the chunky, sprite-legible silhouette — nothing downstream (walk cycle, outfit colour keys, accessory slots) needs to move, only LEG_HEIGHT / ARM_LENGTH to match the new limb lengths. The walk cycle keeps its shape too — arms swinging opposite each other, counter to the stride.

The two builds. There's no neutral — every figure on this page is one or the other. They differ only in the torso silhouette and the hip / arm-attach / leg-stance widths: masc widens the upper torso and narrows the hip; femme narrows the shoulders, cuts the waist and widens the hip. Same head, limbs, height, and walk.

2  Walk cycle

The settled gait, live. swing = sin(walk_phase) drives the legs (±16° about the hip, forward foot lifting); the arms swing opposite each other and counter to the stride — the natural human walk — with a small body dip at the extremes.

This changes the current in-game _arm_swing, which sways both arms the same way. Everything else in Person._leg_stance / _arm_swing stays — same phase variable, same amplitudes, same body bob.

Reduced-motion: the animation starts paused.

3  Facing

The figure reads three-quarter — the feet point the way it faces and the head is turned that way too (features clustered to that side, near ear showing). As built it faces left; face: -1 mirrors the whole group to face right — in game this is Person.facing.

Left is the model as built. Right is the same figure with translate(132,0) scale(-1,1) on the group. Shown mid-stride so the swinging limbs and the foot direction read.

4  Hair & skin

Hairstyles on the Grounded head — the same egg-shaped head, ears and features the full figure draws, at a different scale — each with a different skin token. Flat single-colour fills. Each shape parallels the skull with a per-style offset — close at the temples, more over the crown — and its hairline tucks just inside the skull so it sits on the head. Distinct shapes: a rounded crop, a real side part, curly clumps, a slicked bun / ponytail, an M-recession, a full framing long. Kade is the flush buzz-cut shadow — one crown-clipped fill, no volume.
