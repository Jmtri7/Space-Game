Four proposed cultures beyond the default three

Past the Reach

The default story has three cultures: the Vherathi grow their hulls, the Drossholt bolt theirs, the Sol Federation issues its own to spec. This atlas sketches four more for the edge of the map — one ship and one outfit each — so the silhouettes can be compared before any of it is built.

None of this is in the game. Every plate is a MOCKUP. The four cultures.json entries, the ships, and the outfits are proposals; the ship silhouettes could be extracted into parts the same way Resin & Rivets was, but the outfit redraws would wait on a parts-style figure renderer (same as the Standard Issue outfit chapters). Palettes here are the ones proposed for each cultures.json entry.
Left — specimen
Ship top-down (240×200) or figure front-view (160×200) on void-black, so silhouettes and palettes compare directly.
Shapes only
Every specimen is <polygon> + <circle>, no stroke — outline is a larger offset shape behind, curves are many-sided polygons, the exhaust jet is a class="flame" shape that stays procedural in-engine.
Identity spread
Each chapter opens with the proposed palette as named hex swatches keyed to cultures.json fields, and a numbered rubric of silhouette directives pulled from that culture's theme string.
MOCKUP
Nothing here is wired in. Spec blocks name real fields but are suggestions, never patches.

Chapter 01 — Deeprock Mining Consortium

Built around the job

Already a faction in pilots.json (Oskar Lindqvist, mining_foreman) with no look yet. The outfit that chews ore out of the belts: hardware built around the job it does, painted only where a warning is needed.

hull / wall#5C524A
hull shadow#413A34
glass / lights#FFE078
thrust#FF9646
trim#D6B03C

cultures.json → deeprock — proposed, not in config

  1. Built around the job. One dominant forward volume - an intake maw or ore scoop - a fat tank body behind it, and small ancillary boxes (cabin, mast) clamped on wherever they cleared the load. Front-heavy and asymmetric, not tidy.
  2. Almost no windows. Light is a few harsh floodlights clustered at the working end, drawn from glass_color. The crew works by them, not by a view.
  3. Hazard, not livery. Trim-yellow striping (wall_trim_color) edges every intake, hatch and thruster. It's a warning, not a paint job - everything else stays bare metal_color.
  4. Heavy industrial plant. Blunt rectangular housings, visible rivets, mirrored thruster pairs at the back. It reads as machinery that happens to fly.

Chapter 02 — The Ashfall Rite

The ember in the stone

A close order that keeps to the burnt worlds. Where the Vherathi grow their hulls and the Drossholt bolt theirs, the Kessari fire theirs - single dark slabs of basalt-ceramic, no ornament, lit only by the seam where the stone is still cooling.

hull / wall#22201E
hull shadow#17161A
glass / lights#FF823C
thrust#DC4628
trim#C8602C

cultures.json → kessari — proposed, not in config

  1. One solid mass. A tall blunt slab, wider and heavier toward the base like a standing stone. Roughly bilaterally symmetric but never mechanically precise - one edge is always slightly hand-irregular.
  2. The ember seam. The single defining feature: a thin bright line of glass_color tracing the hull's central spine end to end, plus a matching glow at each thruster. Everything else is near-black metal_color.
  3. Almost sealed. No panel lines, no patch plates, no rows of windows - at most one or two tiny apertures near the top.
  4. Cell, not cabin. Interiors are narrow, dim, high-contrast: black floor and walls, one bright line of light down the centre of every room, rooms kept small and rectangular.

Chapter 03 — The Meridian Free Ports

Seen arriving

A loose confederation of independent trading ports, rich and fond of showing it. Stacked rounded tiers in brass and cream, arched windows because a Meridian captain wants to be seen arriving.

hull / wall#96744A
hull shadow#6A5030
glass / lights#FFEEC8
thrust#FFD282
trim#E8CE96

cultures.json → meridian — proposed, not in config

  1. Stacked tiers. Two or three rounded volumes of decreasing size, each with a scalloped or fluted rim, symmetric and centred - not the Vherathi organic asymmetry.
  2. Ornament is the point. Repeated edge detailing, brass trim (wall_trim_color) framing every opening, a lantern finial at the nose.
  3. Windows on show. Plentiful, in even arched rows along each tier - a Meridian captain wants to be seen arriving.
  4. Warm and bright inside. Wide oval or many-sided rooms joined by broad archways, gold trim on every edge - the opposite of a Kessari cell.

Chapter 04 — The Theln Drift

Never makes port

Nomads who never make port for long. Ships are tensioned membrane over a light exoskeleton - translucent, kite-like, asymmetric - with running lights strung along the rigging.

hull / wall#C8C0B0
hull shadow#9A9384
glass / lights#7FE8E8
thrust#6FD0D0
trim#BFE6DF

cultures.json → theln — proposed, not in config

  1. Membrane on frame. Tensioned translucent sail (low-opacity wall_trim_color) over a light exoskeleton of thin metal_color struts. Kite-like, never a solid hull.
  2. Asymmetric spars. Booms and struts of uneven length - one side always reaches further than the other.
  3. Lights on cables. Running lights (glass_color) strung in lines along the boom edges, like fairy lights on rigging.
  4. Tent, not room. Interiors are soft and low-walled, hanging lights, fabric dividers - a camp that packs down, not a built space.

Wiring in

cultures.json

Four new entries (deeprock, kessari, meridian, theln) — name, description, the colour keys shown, an interior_decoration generator, and a theme string. Self-contained; assets reference them by key.

ship_types.json + graphics.json

One ship type + graphics entry per culture. A ship silhouette can be extracted from its plate into parts via extract_atlas.py / apply_parts.py — add a row to the T table. Theln's low-opacity membrane has no engine support yet (Ship.draw draws flat opaque polygons) — it would ship as solid tinted panels until a translucency layer exists.

graphics.json → outfits

One outfit entry per culture, each just a set of *_color keys on the shared body. The redraws here are mockups — Person.draw is colour-key driven, so a new outfit that only recolours + toggles existing accessory pieces is buildable now; a new silhouette is not.

routines & pilots

Deeprock and Theln want behaviours that don't exist yet (a mining loop, a drift). See the proposed MiningRoutine / PicketRoutine etc. — a culture is only half a faction without a routine and a pilot roster.

Adding cultures is additive, but adding ship types and outfits is not free. A new ship type shows up in every shipyard list and a new outfit in every outfitter — an old save loading into a shop sees the new stock. That's a story-version bump (see SAVE_SYSTEM.md), not a breakage: NPCs and outfits are rebuilt from story config on load, never stored.

Companion atlases: Resin & Rivets (Vherathi + Drossholt) and Standard Issue (the shared body + Sol Federation). Same visual system, same strokeless rule, same figure_parts body.
