Default story — culture design atlas

Not grown, not scavenged — issued

The civil authority that runs the neutral stations and licenses the ships. Where the Vherathi grow their hardware and the Drossholt bolt theirs, the Federation issues it — factory-painted, colour-coded, built from interchangeable modules to one spec and stencilled with a registration number.

The standard_issue culture shipped (config/stories/default, story 1.10.0) — Procyon Ring station, Garrison Row moon, every ship / station / building silhouette extracted into parts. The hardware plates below are those shipped SVGs, and the crew outfit redraws now ship too — the Federation signature (visor slit, centreline stripe, SF-### stencil) is baked into figure_signatures.py.

cultures.json → standard_issue

  1. Prefab modules. A hull is a spine of identical bays; a station is identical rings on a spine; a building is identical floors stacked. Interchangeable, replaceable, numbered.
  2. One corner radius. Rounded rectangles everywhere — a consistent small fillet, not the Vherathi organic curve or the Drossholt raw right angle.
  3. Painted livery. Body colour + a white contrast stripe along the centreline + amber hazard chevrons at every thruster, hatch and edge; a stencilled SF-### on the hull.
  4. Clean symmetry. Mirrored, flush, evenly spaced — no visible patch plates, windows in a regular grid.

Wiring in

Hardware — shipped

Ships (graphics.json), station (graphics.json → space_stations), buildings (building_types.json), furniture, decorations and both layouts are in config/stories/default, silhouettes extracted from these plates into parts by apply_parts.py.

Outfits — the redraw

Each outfit's recoloured base is a graphics.json → outfits entry with a "signature" key. build_figure_signatures.py bakes each signature function's geometry into game/world/figure_signatures.py, and Person.draw() emits it behind / over the body. Shipped for all three cultures + Common Kit.

The body

The cinched waist, narrow rounded shoulders, domed arm tops and the Grounded face kit (oval eyes, straight brow, under-nose shadow, D-ears) are shipped in person_figure.py. See the Common Kit atlas.

Retires

This atlas + Common Kit (+ the other culture atlases) replaced the old combined atlases. Same visual system, same strokeless rule, same body.
