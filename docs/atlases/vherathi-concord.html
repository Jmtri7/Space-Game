Default story — culture design atlas

Ships and cities grown, not assembled

A spacefaring people whose ships and cities are grown from living resin-crystal rather than assembled from plate metal. The Vherathi shape architecture the way coral shapes a reef: asymmetric, load-bearing ridges tapering toward one dominant point, with structural stress traced by thin veins of bioluminescent glass instead of punched-out windows.

The vherathi culture shipped (story 1.10.0) — Alpha Station and its moon rebuilt to the grown floor-plan model, every hull / building / furniture silhouette extracted into parts. The hardware plates below are those shipped SVGs, and the outfit redraws now ship too — the eye-bubble helm clusters and resin-bead glow are baked into figure_signatures.py.

cultures.json → vherathi

  1. Grown, not machined. Tapering asymmetric silhouettes — one side always subtly longer or sharper than the other, echoing a grown crystal, never a mirror-symmetric machined part.
  2. Veins, not windows. No uniform window grid; a handful of lit apertures clustered off-centre along the tallest ridge, tracing a vein of light through the material (glass_color).
  3. Odd counts. Thrusters and fittings mounted in odd counts or uneven spacing where possible, not perfectly mirrored pairs.
  4. One grown structure. Interiors are rounded and flowing — many-sided circle rooms and wide organic corridors joined by overlap; each room's rim traced with a thread of bioluminescent glass.

Wiring in

Hardware — shipped

Ships (graphics.json), station (graphics.json → space_stations), buildings (building_types.json), furniture, decorations and both layouts are in config/stories/default, silhouettes extracted from these plates into parts by apply_parts.py.

Outfits — the redraw

Each outfit's recoloured base is a graphics.json → outfits entry with a "signature" key. build_figure_signatures.py bakes each signature function's geometry into game/world/figure_signatures.py, and Person.draw() emits it behind / over the body. Shipped for all three cultures + Common Kit.

The body

The cinched waist, narrow rounded shoulders, domed arm tops and the Grounded face kit (oval eyes, straight brow, under-nose shadow, D-ears) are shipped in person_figure.py. See the Common Kit atlas.

Retires

This atlas + Common Kit (+ the other culture atlases) replaced the old combined atlases. Same visual system, same strokeless rule, same body.
